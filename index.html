Hermes Household

A private assistant for one household. It is not offered to anyone else.

Hermes Household is a self-hosted assistant that runs on a server at home. The two people in the household use it to keep track of their shared mail, calendar and documents.

What it does with Google

It connects to one Google account, owned by the household, and uses Google's APIs to:

  • read and triage mail in that account, and send mail when a household member approves it;
  • read and update the household calendar;
  • read and edit the household's own files in Drive, Docs and Sheets;
  • look up the account's contacts.

Every change in the Google account needs a household member's approval first. The assistant never connects to anyone else's account.
