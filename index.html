Hermes Homestead

A private assistant for one homestead. It is not offered to anyone else.

Hermes Homestead is a self-hosted assistant that runs on a server at home. The two people in the homestead use it to keep track of their shared mail, calendar and documents.

What it does with Google

It connects to one Google account, owned by the homestead, and uses Google's APIs to:

  • read and triage mail in that account, and send mail when a homestead member approves it;
  • read and update the homestead calendar;
  • read and edit the homestead's own files in Drive, Docs and Sheets;
  • look up the account's contacts.

Every change in the Google account needs a homestead member's approval first. The assistant never connects to anyone else's account.
